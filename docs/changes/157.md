- **Repeated token histories computed once on an Apple M4 Pro** (`--mode mlx`, basal-2.0-mini, -MLX-8bit and
  4.5B-MLX-8bit; answers unchanged): in a batch of prompts, the norms, projections and MLPs skip padding and compute a
  token once when rows share its full history; attention and the readouts keep the padded batch. Where fewer rows
  would make MLX split a projection's sum differently, that projection keeps the original order, and at load a ragged
  pair is checked against the padded forward. M4 Pro, MLX 0.32.3 / mlx-lm 0.32.0, mini-MLX-8bit: 48 one-question
  tickets from 16 clients 5.12 -> 3.27 s (4.5B-MLX-8bit 15.40 -> 10.02 s), eight questions over one ticket 439 -> 350
  ms, the README's two questions 121 -> 107 ms; 500 labelled tickets on each of the three models give the same bits
  as before. Other chips and versions use the regular forward. Contributed by Michał Hachuła (@preacherxp),
  [#157](https://github.com/rkinas/basal/pull/157).
