- **fp8 measured on a Dell Pro Max with GB10** ([HARDWARE.md](HARDWARE.md#dell-pro-max-with-gb10-fp8-with-basal-20)):
  on a batch of 800 decisions the `-FP8` checkpoints of basal-2.0 in `fast-nocompile` take 0.64× (4.5B) and 0.75×
  (mini) of bf16's time, while `--mode fp8` is slower than bf16 there; a server answers one request 3.1–3.4× faster
  (4.5B), and under 64 clients the `-FP8` checkpoint holds 5.6 decisions/s against 3.8 for `--mode fp8`. The batch
  flags are measured again with basal 2.8.0: the defaults stay the best choice. Contributed by Dariusz Koryto
  (@dkoryto), [#139](https://github.com/rkinas/basal/pull/139).
