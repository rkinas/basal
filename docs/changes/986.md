- **How far concurrent clients go over 3k-token states** ([HARDWARE.md](HARDWARE.md#concurrent-clients-over-3k-token-states-basal-omni)):
  basal-omni-1.0-mini, -ultra-FP8 and -ultra (bf16) on an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, `basal-loadtest --synthetic 3000`
  at 1 to 32 clients, with and without `--deterministic`. Throughput stays flat from one client to 32, since one
  3k-token prefill keeps the GPU busy, and `--deterministic` costs nothing at that length. The table comes with the
  command to measure it on another GPU. Asked by @patrykorwat, [#136](https://github.com/rkinas/basal/issues/136).
